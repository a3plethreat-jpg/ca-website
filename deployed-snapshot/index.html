    Our history
    Founded in Porac, Pampanga by Coach Allen Cano, after seeing talented kids priced out of good coaching.
    Mission
    Developing kids and young athletes through discipline, teamwork, confidence, and basketball fundamentals.
    Training venues
    Pio, Porac · Fiesta Community, Porac · Sta. Trinidad, Angeles City — all in Pampanga
    Competition divisions
    11U, 12U, 13U, 14U, 17U
    Training schedule
    To be announced
    Programs
    Group camps, plus 1-on-1 and group clinics with Coach Allen Cano (On Stage, 3rd Floor, Robinsons Balibago). Built on fundamentals, film, and reps. No pay-to-play.

    Coaches & achievements

    LEADERS WHO BUILD CHAMPIONS.

    Head Coach & Founder · Porac, Pampanga

    Allen Cano

    Founder of Coach-A Triple Threat. Builds complete players through fundamentals, film, and relentless reps — developing MVPs, not chasing them.

    Background: Started Triple Threat in Porac, Pampanga after seeing talented kids priced out of good coaching. Sessions focus on fundamentals, footwork, and basketball IQ.

    Philosophy: Full commitment, respect, hard work, and the willingness to improve every day.

    • Best Coach — NYBL Central Luzon Championship – Bulacan, Season 4 Invitational Cup · 17U Division
    • Champion — NYBL Central Luzon Championship – Bulacan, Season 4 Invitational Cup · 17U Division
